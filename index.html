Module 01

The Hook Deconstruction

Examine the opening rhetorical device. Click the underlined terms to reveal analytical insights.

Imagine this. You just bought a brand-new, super expensive pair of sneakers. You show them to your best friend, and their first reaction is: ‘Whoa, bro, that’s sick.’

Now, pause for a second. If you translate that literally, your friend just looked at your beautiful new shoes and said they have a disease. That makes zero sense, right? But we all know exactly what they mean: those shoes are incredibly cool.

Or think about this one. You’re playing a game, and someone is hiding in the corner acting weird. You don’t stand up and formally announce, 'Excuse me, I believe you are behaving in a highly suspicious manner.' No! Nobody has time for that. You just point and say, ‘You’re acting sus.’

We use these terms daily. But who governs these unwritten rules? Let's investigate the underlying mechanics.
